# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for
everyone. Participation is open to people of every background and experience
level who engage in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without attacking people.
- Give and accept actionable feedback gracefully.
- Respect different viewpoints, experiences, and communication styles.
- Credit other people's work and protect private information.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or unwanted attention.
- Discriminatory, sexualized, insulting, or demeaning language or imagery.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or sustained disruption of project spaces.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Impersonation, deliberate deception, or retaliation against a reporter.
- Any other behavior that a reasonable person would consider unsafe or
  inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository interactions and other spaces where someone
officially represents the project, including issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and project events.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report possible violations privately through a contact method listed on the
[LLMNetOps organization profile](https://github.com/LLMNetOps). If no private
contact method is available, open an issue in
[LLMNetOps/clab-mt](https://github.com/LLMNetOps/clab-mt/issues/new) asking the
maintainers to establish a private channel, but do not include incident details
in that issue.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. The
project maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation,
or ban an individual when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement
decisions will consider context, severity, prior behavior, and the safety of
affected people. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
